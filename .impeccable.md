# WSB design context

Source: the user's supplied WSB product brief, logo and 30 October 2026 planning
request; detailed plan in docs/website-plan/README.md.

Audience: street-dance crews and managers, dancers including minors and guardians,
spectators, chapter directors, reviewers, finance staff, judges, marshals and
venue operators across countries. Use contexts include phones during registration,
tablets in dark venues, desktop administration and arena screens.

Jobs: discover events, register and prepare crews, manage eligibility/consent and
payments, judge reliably during outages, operate the stage and publish results.

Requested personality: professional, expressive street-dance championship identity
with consistent themes across pages. Proposed design words: defiant, collective,
precise. The supplied black/white crown and graffiti mark remains intact.
Existing WSB surface system and charcoal/chrome/volt tokens govern visual work.

Public pages may be cinematic; operational screens prioritize accurate state and
accessible tasks. All ordinary pages support portrait/landscape, small viewports
and zoom. A fixed-height scoring screen is an optimization, not a clipping rule.

Reference and font choices are proposals documented in the plan, not final
brand-owner approval. Real media, official rules and policy content require
accountable source approval before release.
